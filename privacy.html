Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy describes the Airspace Reservations application operated by the owner of ticketop1@gmail.com. It covers the internal reservation workflow and this informational website.

1. Data the application accesses

  • Google account information: the Gmail account’s email address, used to verify that the intended account authorized access.
  • Email data: message identifiers, timestamps, sender and subject information, and message contents needed to identify and process relevant verification or reservation-confirmation emails.
  • Authorization data: OAuth client information, refresh tokens, and temporary access tokens used to call Gmail APIs.
  • Operational records: visit dates and times, ticket quantities, workflow status, reservation numbers, and diagnostic information about execution.

The Gmail permissions requested are gmail.readonly and gmail.send. Read-only access technically covers the mailbox, although workflow processing targets relevant messages. The application does not request permission to delete or modify existing messages.

2. How data is used

Google account data is used to authenticate the authorized mailbox, obtain newly issued verification codes, support checks of reservation-confirmation messages, and send operational reports and alerts. Reservation records help track completed orders and prevent accidental duplicate submissions.

Google account data is not sold, used for advertising or profiling, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. Storage and protection

Initial Google authorization is completed on the operator’s computer. The setup tool writes an OAuth credentials file locally. The operator stores the authorized credentials in GitHub Actions Secrets for use by the workflow. Temporary access tokens and relevant email contents are processed in memory during execution. The application is designed not to print verification codes, tokens, or email bodies in workflow logs.

Reservation state is stored in a local SQLite database on the operator-controlled runner. Backups are created locally and uploaded as artifacts in the private GitHub repository. Workflow logs and email reports contain operational status and may contain reservation numbers. Google account passwords are not collected by the application.

Access to repository settings, secrets, runner files, backups, and reports is controlled by the operator. Files downloaded or copied by the operator may also be subject to the operator’s device-backup or synchronization settings. No storage or transmission system is completely secure.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Google processes authorization requests and Gmail API operations. GitHub processes source code, workflow execution, stored secrets, logs, and uploaded backups. The operator-controlled runner executes the browser workflow. The museum receives the information submitted through its own reservation service, including its verification code when needed to complete sign-in.

The application does not intentionally disclose mailbox contents or credentials to unrelated third parties. Access may occur where necessary to operate or secure the workflow, at the account owner’s direction, or where legally required. The account owner can inspect relevant emails directly through Gmail.

5. Retention and deletion

Relevant email contents and verification codes are processed transiently by the application; the workflow does not intentionally archive complete email messages. Source emails and sent reports remain in Gmail until deleted under the account owner’s settings.

OAuth credentials remain in local setup files and GitHub Secrets until removed or replaced by the operator. Reservation records and local backups are retained for operational continuity until the operator deletes them. Uploaded booking-ledger artifacts are configured for 90-day retention, subject to GitHub’s effective settings. Workflow logs follow the repository’s configured retention policy.

The account owner may revoke Google access and remove local credential files and GitHub Secrets. Deletion of reservation records or backups requires stopping the workflow and reconciling existing bookings first to avoid duplicate orders. For assistance, contact ticketop1@gmail.com. Deletion from the application does not cancel museum reservations or delete records held by Google, GitHub, or the museum under their own policies.

6. Your choices

Google access is granted through an explicit authorization process. The account owner can revoke access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored operational records. The operator can stop the runner and disable workflows independently.

7. Google API Limited Use

Airspace Reservations’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. This informational website

These static pages have no application login, forms, advertising, or analytics scripts. They do not request Google authorization or run the reservation workflow. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process standard website request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links lead to services with their own privacy policies.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when application behavior or service providers change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, contact ticketop1@gmail.com.
